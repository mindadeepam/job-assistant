# Browser

- **The session is the user's.** Never sign out, change account settings or credentials, or do anything beyond the task. A sign-in page means stop and queue it for the user.
- **Tabs:** keep few open, about one per parallel task. Close each tab you opened as soon as you're done with it. Never close tabs you didn't open: the user may keep signed-in tabs in a group, and in Chrome closing a group's first tab can dissolve the group.
- **Work left for the user:** a form filled to the last step can stay open in its tab for them to submit. Keep it to a handful (about five, unless the user wants more) and list each in the needs-you queue. For outreach drafts, put the profile link and the note text in the queue instead of leaving typed tabs: queued text survives a closed browser.
- **Reading:** prefer the page's text or accessibility tree to screenshots. They're faster and catch text that's off screen.
- **Lazy lists** (LinkedIn messages, infinite job lists): content outside the viewport may be empty. Scroll in small steps and collect as you go.
- **No browser connected:** do what doesn't need one (search, fetch public pages, drafts), and tell the user what was skipped.
